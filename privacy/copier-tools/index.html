Privacy policy: Copier Tools for PU Prime

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Copier Tools for PU Prime ("the extension") adds extra figures to the PU Prime copy-trading pages at myaccount.puprime.com. It is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by PU Prime.

What the extension reads

It reads the positions, balances, swap, commission and trade history that the PU Prime page already shows you, and calculates extra figures from them in your browser. This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the extension stores on your device

Your choices (such as the Buy/Sell filter and whether panels are open), a day-start balance, your copy settings, and your closed-trade history per copy account. This is kept in your browser's local storage for the PU Prime site and never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data for myaccount.puprime.com or removing the extension.

What the extension sends

Once a day it sends a randomly generated ID (made on your first visit, not linked to your name, email, PU Prime account or trades) and the extension version number to https://matrix.yanev.co. The developer uses this only to count how many installs are active. No other data is sent.

What the extension does not do

It does not place, change or close trades. It does not read or send your password, login details or personal details. It does not use advertising or analytics services and does not sell or share data with third parties. It does not download or run code from outside the package. The optional "Buy me a coffee" link simply opens a payment page in a new tab; nothing is sent when it is shown.

Retention

The anonymous ID and version are kept as one record per install per day for usage statistics.
